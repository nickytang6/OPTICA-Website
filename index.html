Cal Poly OPTICA Student Chapter

Founded in 2026, we are still relatively new and growing. Our goals for this club are to promote and spread awareness of opportunities in optics since we don’t have an optics or optical engineering program here at Cal Poly. Being a part of the OPTICA student chapter is a great way to explore careers and pathways into optics. There are countless resources you can access as an OPTICA international member, but all of our club events are completely free still if you do not wish to become an official OPTICA member.
