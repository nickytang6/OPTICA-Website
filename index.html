Cal Poly OPTICA Student Chapter

Founded in 2026, we're Cal Poly's first and only optics club. Started by students researchers at the Cal Poly Photon Lab, our goal is to introduce students to opportunities in optics and optical engineering with community events, workshops, and speaker panels. All of our events are open, even if you're not an OPTICA member.
