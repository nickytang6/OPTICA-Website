What is OPTICA

About OPTICA

Optica (formerly OSA), Advancing Optics and Photonics Worldwide, is the society dedicated to promoting the generation, application, archiving and dissemination of knowledge in the field. Founded in 1916, it is the leading organization for scientists, engineers, business professionals, students and others interested in the science of light. Optica's renowned publications, meetings, online resources and in-person activities fuel discoveries, shape real-life applications and accelerate scientific, technical and educational achievement.

About a student chapter

Optica student chapters give members hands-on access to knowledge sharing and leadership opportunities not available to non-members. Through Optica's Traveling Lecturers program, chapters can invite professionals from a network of nearly 1,000 experts to speak on campus, with lodging and travel costs covered by a grant. Students are also invited to attend Optica's annual meeting, Frontiers in Optics (FiO), where those selected have their transportation, lodging, and conference fees covered, with chapter-affiliated students given priority in the selection process. Beyond these benefits, participating in a chapter helps members build technical expertise, leadership experience, and lasting relationships with peers and mentors in the field.
